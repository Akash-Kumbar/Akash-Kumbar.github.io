I’m Akash Kumbar, a robotics research student working under Prof. K. Madhava Krishna. My work sits at the intersection of 3D computer vision, representation learning, and robotics; building intelligent systems that can perceive, understand, and act in the real world.

I’m deeply curious about how machines see and learn, and I’m driven by the challenge of pushing robotics closer to  or beyond human-level perception and autonomy. Whether it’s designing better representations for vision or exploring new learning methods, my goal is to develop technologies that make robots more capable, adaptable, and impactful.

Beyond research, I’m always excited to collaborate, experiment with new ideas, and contribute to the broader conversation around AI and robotics.
