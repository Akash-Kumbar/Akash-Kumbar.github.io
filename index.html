Akash Kumbar
akash.kumbar@research.iiit.ac.in | itsakashkumbar@gmail.com

I am currently pursuing Master of Science (by Research) at IIIT Hyderabad, where I am a Graduate Student Researcher at the Robotics Research Center under the guidance of Prof. Madhava Krishna.

My work lies at the intersection of 3D computer vision, representation learning, and robotics. I build intelligent systems that can perceive, understand, and act in the real world.

Before joining IIIT Hyderabad, I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at KLE Technological University, Hubballi. I was also an undergraduate researcher at the Center of Excellence in Visual Intelligence under Prof. Uma Mudenagudi, where I worked on refining point-cloud data, primarily through upsampling. This work led to two papers at ICCV 2023 workshops.

Beyond robotics and academia, I practice mixed martial arts and run(practicing for a half marathon 🙌). Feel free to reach out—I'd love to connect!
